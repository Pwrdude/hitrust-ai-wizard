# LC: AI legal and compliance

## LC-01 Identify and evaluate compliance and legal obligations for AI development and deployment

HITRUST ID `19.06aAISecOrganizational.1` · Domain 19 Data Protection & Privacy · Control 06.a Identification of Applicable Legislation  
Inheritable: No · Applies: all model types

> Before deployment and at least annually, the organization assesses its compliance with legal and regulatory requirements for AI development and deployment, including liability for harmful outputs.

Evaluative elements:
- `LC-01[1]` Compliance with applicable AI laws and regulations (including liability for outputs) assessed
- `LC-01[2]` Assessed before deployment
- `LC-01[3]` Assessed at least annually

Note: Dual responsibility.

## LC-02 Identify and evaluate constraints on data used for AI

HITRUST ID `19.06cAISecOrganizational.1` · Domain 19 Data Protection & Privacy · Control 06.c Protection of Organizational Records  
Inheritable: No · Applies: ML models (predictive, generative)

> The organization assesses compliance with constraints on data used for training, validation, tuning, and RAG (laws, regulations, contracts, internal data governance, copyright and commercial interests), acts on the results, and does so before the data is used and at least annually.

Evaluative elements:
- `LC-02[1]` Constraints from laws assessed
- `LC-02[2]` Constraints from regulations assessed
- `LC-02[3]` Contractual obligations assessed
- `LC-02[4]` Internal data governance requirements assessed
- `LC-02[5]` Copyright and commercial constraints assessed
- `LC-02[6]` Results acted on
- `LC-02[7]` Assessed before the data is used for AI
- `LC-02[8]` Reassessed at least annually

Note: ML models only. Dual responsibility.
